CRITICAL NOTE: This split-outcome manipulation tactic applies to ALL types of betting picks — including 1X2 Match Winner, Over/Under Goals, Correct Scores, Both Teams to Score (BTTS), and Asian Handicaps.

1. The Outcome Splitting Trick

If a scammer targets 150 potential victims, they divide them into three groups of 50. Group A gets "Home Team Wins", Group B gets "Draw", and Group C gets "Away Team Wins". Regardless of what happens, one group gets a "100% correct tip."

2. Mock-up/Edited Betslips

Scammers use photoshopped images, specialized editing tools, modified local HTML code, or dummy bookmaker accounts with infinite fake balances to fabricate totally fake proofs showing victorious betting slips.

3. The Severe Legal Realities

In virtually all modern jurisdictions, rigging matches falls under federal anti-racketeering legislation. No real criminal mastermind would compromise a multi-million operation to sell "insiders" for €100.
